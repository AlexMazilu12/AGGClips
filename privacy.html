Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Agg Clips Studio (“the tool”) is a personal desktop application operated by Agg Clips (“we”). It runs on the operator's own computer and is used only to manage and publish the operator's own clips to the operator's own accounts. It is not a public service: no other people sign up for it or connect their accounts to it.

1. Data we access

SourceWhatWhy
TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API) With the operator's permission: the connected account's open ID, display name and avatar (user.info.basic), and the ability to send a video to that account's drafts (video.upload). OAuth access and refresh tokens. To show which account is connected and to upload approved clips as drafts, which the operator then finishes and posts inside the TikTok app.
YouTube (YouTube Data API) With the operator's permission: the ability to upload videos to the operator's channel (youtube.upload). Public data with an API key: live-stream titles, viewer counts and public live-chat messages of tracked channels. To publish approved clips as Shorts and to detect highlights in public live streams.
Instagram (Instagram Graph API) With the operator's permission: the operator's Facebook Page and linked professional Instagram account, and the ability to publish Reels to it. To publish approved clips as Reels.
Twitch, Kick Public stream information (who is live, titles, categories, viewer counts) and, on Twitch, public chat messages read anonymously. To know which streams to record and to detect highlights.

2. How the data is used and stored

  • Everything is processed and stored locally on the operator's computer. There is no server, database or cloud account operated by us that receives this data.
  • OAuth tokens are kept in local files on that computer and are only sent to the platform that issued them, over HTTPS.
  • Public chat messages are analysed in memory to score moments (message rate, laughter, hype). A few short message samples may be saved next to a detected moment so the operator can review it. Usernames are not used for anything else.
  • Stream recordings are kept in a rolling buffer (6 hours by default) and deleted automatically; only the clips the operator chooses to keep remain.
  • Videos are sent only to the platform the operator approved them for.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not access other people's TikTok, YouTube or Instagram accounts.
  • This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens and clips stay on the operator's computer until the operator deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time:

  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions (or Apps and websites) → remove the app.
  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Meta / Instagram: Facebook Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) → remove the app.

Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless; deleting the tool's data folder removes them completely.

5. YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Children

The tool is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly process their personal data.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
